# Siebrt - Quality Standards & Compliance

## Quality Criteria

- **Specific Quality Metrics:**
  - Content accuracy: 98% factual accuracy in all financial data presented.
  - User engagement: Achieve a minimum engagement rate of 15% on all digital platforms.
  - Response time: Customer inquiries responded to within 24 hours, 90% of the time.

- **Measurable Standards:**
  - Content clarity: All content should be understandable at a 10th-grade reading level.
  - Visual content quality: Maintain a minimum resolution of 1080p for all visual media.

- **Acceptance Criteria:**
  - Content must be peer-reviewed by at least two subject matter experts.
  - All promotional materials must align with Siebrt's brand voice and values.

## Review Process

- **Review Workflow and Stages:**
  - Initial draft review by the content team for brand alignment.
  - Technical review by financial experts for accuracy.
  - Final approval by the compliance officer before publication.

- **Reviewer Roles and Responsibilities:**
  - Content Editor: Ensures adherence to brand voice and grammatical correctness.
  - Financial Analyst: Validates the accuracy of financial data and projections.
  - Compliance Officer: Reviews for legal and regulatory compliance.

- **Quality Checkpoints:**
  - Pre-publication checklist completion.
  - Post-publication performance review within one week.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Regulatory Compliance Needs:**
  - Adherence to SEC regulations for all financial publications.
  - Compliance with GDPR standards for user data protection.

- **Industry Standards:**
  - Meet CFA Institute's ethics and standards in all financial advice.
  - Follow FINRA advertising guidelines for all promotional content.

- **Legal Requirements:**
  - Legal review of all contracts and partnerships.
  - Regular audits to ensure compliance with federal and state laws.

## Brand Consistency

- **Brand Alignment Requirements:**
  - Maintain Siebrt’s core brand promise in all communications: "You can have your cake and eat it too."
  - Consistent use of the brand’s voice and tone as outlined in the Brand Voice Framework.

- **Consistency Checkpoints:**
  - Quarterly brand audit to evaluate consistency across all channels.
  - Use of approved templates and style guides in all communications.

- **Brand Compliance Metrics:**
  - Achieve a 95% compliance rate in brand audits.
  - Maintain a Net Promoter Score (NPS) of 70 or above for brand perception.

## Performance Metrics

- **Key Performance Indicators:**
  - Increase in newsletter subscriptions by 20% annually.
  - Customer satisfaction score of 85% or above in quarterly surveys.

- **Quality Measurement Methods:**
  - Use analytics tools to measure user engagement and content reach.
  - Conduct bi-annual focus groups for qualitative feedback.

- **Reporting and Tracking:**
  - Monthly performance reports to senior management.
  - Real-time tracking of key metrics with dashboards.

## Error Prevention

- **Common Error Patterns:**
  - Misinformation in financial data.
  - Misalignment with brand voice.

- **Prevention Strategies:**
  - Implement a double-review system for all content.
  - Regular training sessions on brand voice and compliance standards.

- **Quality Assurance Protocols:**
  - Conduct quarterly training updates on compliance and quality standards.
  - Establish a feedback loop for continuous improvement based on error analysis.
