# Siebrt - Content Production Guide

## Content Strategy

### Strategic Approach to Content
- **Objective-Driven:** Focus on creating content that educates and empowers Gen Z and Millennial audiences, aligning with their financial aspirations and challenges.
- **Audience-Centric:** Tailor content to meet the preferences and pain points of digital natives and career-focused professionals.
- **Value-Driven:** Incorporate transparency and social responsibility to resonate with audience values.

### Content Objectives and Goals
- **Educate:** Simplify financial concepts for better understanding and actionability.
- **Engage:** Use interactive and culturally relevant content to maintain interest.
- **Empower:** Build confidence in financial decision-making through relatable examples and success stories.

### Alignment with Business Objectives
- **Brand Visibility:** Increase Siebrt's presence as a thought leader in financial education.
- **Audience Growth:** Expand reach and engagement through targeted content strategies.
- **Customer Loyalty:** Foster trust and long-term relationships with audiences by delivering consistent value.

## Content Types & Formats

### Preferred Content Formats
- **Articles and Blogs:** In-depth yet accessible pieces on financial topics.
- **Videos and Webinars:** Engaging visual content for complex concepts.
- **Infographics:** Visual aids for quick comprehension of data and trends.
- **Podcasts:** Conversational formats for audience engagement.

### Channel-Specific Guidelines
- **Social Media:** Short, impactful posts with visuals and trending hashtags.
- **Email Newsletters:** Personalized, scannable content with clear CTAs.
- **Website:** SEO-optimized, easy-to-navigate content with a focus on user experience.

### Content Hierarchy and Structure
- **Headlines:** Clear, compelling, and keyword-rich.
- **Introduction:** Hook the audience with relatable scenarios or questions.
- **Body:** Break down information into digestible sections with bullet points.
- **Conclusion:** Summarize key takeaways and encourage next steps.

## Production Workflow

### Step-by-Step Production Process
1. **Ideation:** Brainstorm content ideas based on audience needs and current trends.
2. **Research:** Gather insights and data to support content topics.
3. **Drafting:** Develop initial content drafts following style guidelines.
4. **Review:** Peer review for accuracy, clarity, and alignment with brand voice.
5. **Editing:** Refine content for quality and coherence.
6. **Approval:** Obtain final approval from stakeholders.
7. **Publishing:** Schedule content across designated channels.

### Roles and Responsibilities
- **Content Strategist:** Oversees content planning and alignment with goals.
- **Writer:** Creates drafts and refines content.
- **Editor:** Reviews and enhances content quality.
- **Designer:** Develops visual elements and layout.
- **Project Manager:** Coordinates timeline and ensures milestone completion.

### Timeline and Milestones
- **Weekly Check-ins:** Regular updates on content progress.
- **Monthly Review:** Assess performance and adjust strategies.
- **Quarterly Planning:** Evaluate past content success and plan future initiatives.

## Style Guidelines

### Writing Style Requirements
- **Conversational:** Use a friendly, approachable tone.
- **Educational:** Explain complex terms with analogies and examples.
- **Empowering:** Focus on potential and achievable actions.

### Formatting Standards
- **Consistency:** Uniform use of headers, bullet points, and typography.
- **Scannability:** Short paragraphs, subheadings, and visual breaks.

### Visual and Design Guidelines
- **Brand Colors and Fonts:** Adhere to brand identity for consistency.
- **Imagery:** Use high-quality, relevant visuals that support content.

## Content Calendar & Planning

### Planning Methodology
- **Content Brainstorming Sessions:** Regular team meetings to generate ideas.
- **Audience Feedback:** Incorporate audience insights and feedback into planning.

### Content Scheduling Approach
- **Monthly Calendar:** Map out content topics and publishing dates.
- **Flexibility:** Allow room for timely content based on current events.

### Review and Approval Process
- **Stage-Gate Reviews:** Implement checkpoints for feedback and revisions.
- **Final Approval:** Secure sign-off from necessary stakeholders before publishing.

## Tools & Resources

### Recommended Tools and Platforms
- **Content Management System (CMS):** WordPress or similar platforms for content hosting.
- **Design Tools:** Canva or Adobe Creative Suite for visual content.
- **Analytics Tools:** Google Analytics for performance tracking.

### Templates and Resources
- **Content Templates:** Pre-designed templates for consistency and efficiency.
- **Style Guide:** Comprehensive guide outlining voice, tone, and formatting.

### Training and Support Materials
- **Workshops:** Regular training sessions on new tools and best practices.
- **Documentation:** Access to guides and resources for continuous learning.
