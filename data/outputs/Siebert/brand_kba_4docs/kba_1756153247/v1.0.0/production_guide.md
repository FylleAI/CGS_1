# Siebert - Content Production Guide

## Content Strategy

- **Strategic Approach to Content**
  - Focus on creating content that resonates with Gen Z and Millennial professionals.
  - Ensure content independence while aligning with Siebert's brand identity and mission.
  - Foster a community-oriented approach, emphasizing peer-to-peer learning and authentic engagement.

- **Content Objectives and Goals**
  - Educate and empower Gen Z and Millennials on financial literacy.
  - Enhance brand visibility by integrating financial education with cultural trends.
  - Build trust and community by providing actionable financial advice.

- **Alignment with Business Objectives**
  - Support Siebert's mission of making financial services accessible and relatable.
  - Promote the "YOUR FUTURE YOUR MOVE" campaign by aligning content with its themes.
  - Highlight Siebert's unique value proposition: enjoying small luxuries while being financially responsible.

## Content Types & Formats

- **Preferred Content Formats**
  - Blog posts, newsletters, and social media content with a focus on visual and scannable formats.
  - Interactive formats like quizzes and polls to engage a mobile-first audience.
  - Video content for platforms like TikTok and Instagram to reach digital natives.

- **Channel-Specific Guidelines**
  - Tailor content tone and style to suit each platform: conversational for social media, educational for blogs.
  - Use visual aids and infographics for complex topics to enhance understanding and retention.

- **Content Hierarchy and Structure**
  - Begin with engaging headlines and summaries to capture attention.
  - Use bullet points, subheadings, and visuals to break up text and improve readability.
  - End with a clear call-to-action, encouraging the audience to explore further or engage with the content.

## Production Workflow

- **Step-by-Step Production Process**
  1. **Ideation:** Brainstorm content ideas aligned with current trends and audience interests.
  2. **Research:** Conduct thorough research to ensure accuracy and relevance.
  3. **Drafting:** Create initial drafts, focusing on clarity and engagement.
  4. **Review:** Peer review and editing to maintain quality and brand consistency.
  5. **Approval:** Final approval from content managers or stakeholders.
  6. **Publication:** Schedule and publish content across appropriate channels.

- **Roles and Responsibilities**
  - **Content Creators:** Develop and draft content.
  - **Editors:** Ensure content quality and adherence to brand guidelines.
  - **Content Managers:** Oversee the content strategy and approve final drafts.
  - **Designers:** Create visual elements and ensure aesthetic consistency.

- **Timeline and Milestones**
  - Set clear deadlines for each stage of the production process.
  - Use project management tools to track progress and ensure timely completion.

## Style Guidelines

- **Writing Style Requirements**
  - Maintain a conversational and approachable tone, avoiding jargon and complex language.
  - Use relatable analogies and cultural references to explain financial concepts.

- **Formatting Standards**
  - Use short paragraphs and bullet points for easy scanning.
  - Incorporate emojis and visuals where appropriate to enhance engagement.

- **Visual and Design Guidelines**
  - Follow a consistent color palette and typography that aligns with Siebert's brand identity.
  - Use high-quality images and graphics to support content and improve visual appeal.

## Content Calendar & Planning

- **Planning Methodology**
  - Regularly review audience insights and market trends to guide content planning.
  - Collaborate with cross-functional teams to align content with broader marketing initiatives.

- **Content Scheduling Approach**
  - Develop a monthly content calendar to ensure a steady flow of content.
  - Optimize posting times based on audience engagement metrics.

- **Review and Approval Process**
  - Implement a standardized review process to ensure content accuracy and quality.
  - Establish clear criteria for content approval to streamline the workflow.

## Tools & Resources

- **Recommended Tools and Platforms**
  - Use content management systems like WordPress for blog management.
  - Leverage social media management tools like Hootsuite for scheduling and analytics.

- **Templates and Resources**
  - Provide content templates for consistency in format and style.
  - Develop a brand guide detailing voice, tone, and visual identity.

- **Training and Support Materials**
  - Offer training sessions on content strategy and audience engagement.
  - Create reference materials on financial topics and brand messaging for content creators.
