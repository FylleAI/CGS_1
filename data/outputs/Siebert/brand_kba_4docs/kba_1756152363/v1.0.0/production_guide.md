# Siebert - Content Production Guide

## Content Strategy
- **Strategic Approach to Content**
  - Focus on creating content that resonates with Gen Z and Millennial Professionals by addressing their unique financial challenges and preferences.
  - Leverage independent editorial perspectives to maintain authenticity and trust.
  - Highlight Siebert’s core brand promise, "You can have your cake and eat it too," in content to align with the audience's desire for financial responsibility without sacrificing enjoyment.

- **Content Objectives and Goals**
  - Educate the audience on financial literacy while making the content engaging and relatable.
  - Enhance brand visibility and positioning as a thought leader in financial education for younger demographics.
  - Drive engagement through community-centric, peer-to-peer learning content.

- **Alignment with Business Objectives**
  - Align content objectives with Siebert’s marketing campaigns like "YOUR FUTURE YOUR MOVE" to ensure cohesive messaging.
  - Support the "Future Millionaires Newsletter" project to expand its reach and impact.

## Content Types & Formats
- **Preferred Content Formats**
  - Articles, newsletters, and social media posts with a focus on visual content like infographics and videos.
  - Emphasize mobile-first content that is scannable, using bullet points, emojis, and short paragraphs.

- **Channel-Specific Guidelines**
  - Utilize TikTok, Instagram, and Reddit for engaging short-form content aimed at Gen Z.
  - LinkedIn and blog articles for in-depth analysis targeting Millennial Professionals.

- **Content Hierarchy and Structure**
  - Prioritize educational content that is practical and actionable, followed by thought leadership pieces.
  - Use a consistent structure: introduce the topic, provide actionable advice, and conclude with a call-to-action.

## Production Workflow
- **Step-by-Step Production Process**
  1. **Ideation:** Gather input from team and audience insights.
  2. **Research:** Conduct thorough research to ensure content accuracy and relevance.
  3. **Drafting:** Develop content drafts adhering to brand voice and style guidelines.
  4. **Review & Edit:** Peer review for feedback, followed by editing for clarity and coherence.
  5. **Approval:** Final approval from content manager or editor-in-chief.
  6. **Publishing:** Schedule and publish content across relevant channels.

- **Roles and Responsibilities**
  - **Content Creator:** Responsible for drafting and initial edits.
  - **Editor:** Provides feedback and ensures content aligns with brand standards.
  - **Content Manager:** Oversees the entire content production process and final approvals.

- **Timeline and Milestones**
  - Establish a timeline with specific milestones, from ideation to publishing, ensuring timely delivery of content.

## Style Guidelines
- **Writing Style Requirements**
  - Maintain a conversational and culturally relevant tone.
  - Avoid jargon and complex terminology; use relatable analogies.
  - Be empowering and confidence-building, focusing on achievable actions.

- **Formatting Standards**
  - Use headings, subheadings, and bullet points for easy navigation.
  - Incorporate visuals to enhance understanding and engagement.

- **Visual and Design Guidelines**
  - Use a consistent color palette and typography aligned with Siebert’s brand.
  - Ensure visuals are high-quality, relevant, and enhance the content’s message.

## Content Calendar & Planning
- **Planning Methodology**
  - Use a collaborative approach to content planning with regular brainstorming sessions.
  - Develop quarterly content themes that align with broader marketing strategies.

- **Content Scheduling Approach**
  - Utilize a digital calendar for scheduling content releases, ensuring a consistent flow.
  - Schedule content based on audience peak engagement times.

- **Review and Approval Process**
  - Implement a structured review process, allowing sufficient time for feedback and revisions.
  - Ensure final approval is obtained before scheduling content for publication.

## Tools & Resources
- **Recommended Tools and Platforms**
  - Project management tools like Trello or Asana for workflow tracking.
  - Grammarly and Hemingway for editing and readability improvements.

- **Templates and Resources**
  - Provide templates for articles, social media posts, and newsletters.
  - Develop a repository of stock images and graphics that adhere to brand guidelines.

- **Training and Support Materials**
  - Offer training sessions on content creation best practices and platform-specific techniques.
  - Create a support network for content creators to share insights and troubleshoot challenges.
