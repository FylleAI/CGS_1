# Siebert - Quality Standards & Compliance

## Quality Criteria
- **Specific Quality Metrics**
  - Customer satisfaction score ≥ 85%.
  - Product defect rate ≤ 2% per quarter.
  - Content accuracy rate ≥ 98%.

- **Measurable Standards**
  - Response time to customer inquiries within 24 hours.
  - Financial advice accuracy checked against latest market data monthly.
  - Newsletter publication adherence to bi-weekly schedule.

- **Acceptance Criteria**
  - All services must pass internal quality checks before release.
  - All content must be reviewed by two independent editors.
  - Compliance with established brand voice guidelines.

## Review Process
- **Review Workflow and Stages**
  - Initial draft review by content creator.
  - Secondary review by quality assurance specialist.
  - Final approval by department head.

- **Reviewer Roles and Responsibilities**
  - Content Creator: Ensure initial alignment with brand voice.
  - Quality Assurance Specialist: Verify accuracy and adherence to standards.
  - Department Head: Final gatekeeper for release.

- **Quality Checkpoints**
  - Pre-publication review of all materials.
  - Monthly quality audit reports.
  - Quarterly review meetings to address recurring issues.

## Compliance Requirements
- **Regulatory Compliance Needs**
  - Adherence to SEC regulations for all financial advice.
  - Compliance with GDPR for data protection.

- **Industry Standards**
  - Follow Financial Industry Regulatory Authority (FINRA) guidelines.
  - Compliance with ISO 9001 quality management standards.

- **Legal Requirements**
  - Ensure all contracts are vetted by legal counsel.
  - Adhere to advertising standards and truth-in-advertising laws.

## Brand Consistency
- **Brand Alignment Requirements**
  - All communications must align with the brand's core promise: "You can have your cake and eat it too."
  - Use of approved brand voice characteristics in all content.

- **Consistency Checkpoints**
  - Bi-annual brand voice workshops for all employees.
  - Monthly brand alignment reviews of all published materials.

- **Brand Compliance Metrics**
  - Brand alignment score ≥ 90% in internal audits.
  - Customer feedback on brand consistency ≥ 80% positive.

## Performance Metrics
- **Key Performance Indicators**
  - Customer retention rate ≥ 90%.
  - Increase in Gen Z engagement by 10% annually.

- **Quality Measurement Methods**
  - Regular customer surveys and feedback loops.
  - Use of analytics tools to track content performance.

- **Reporting and Tracking**
  - Monthly reports on key performance metrics.
  - Real-time tracking of customer service response times.

## Error Prevention
- **Common Error Patterns**
  - Misalignment with brand voice.
  - Inaccurate financial data dissemination.

- **Prevention Strategies**
  - Regular training sessions on brand guidelines.
  - Implementation of a double-check system for all published data.

- **Quality Assurance Protocols**
  - Establishment of a cross-functional quality assurance team.
  - Continuous improvement loop through feedback and audits.
